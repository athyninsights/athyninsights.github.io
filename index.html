Athyn Insights was started in 2021 with a singular mission to enhance the data science capabilities of the Department of State. As Secretary Rubio has stated, winning the AI race is non-negotiable. Athyn Insights helps the U.S. Department of State transform its data into actionable insights. We build innovative AI solutions that enable diplomats to work more effectively and make better decisions.

We operate at the meeting point between top-down strategy and bottom-up innovation—solving real-world problems today while building the infrastructure that supports future technology and enterprise-scale solutions. Our flagship platform, Funhouse, is an AI-powered data science innovation lab we built and maintain, now serving over 700 users across 60+ countries and 30+ bureaus. Built on Azure AI Services and OpenAI models, Funhouse harnesses the power of GenAI Computer Vision, Document Intelligence, AI Speech, Vector Embedding, RAG, and more to develop impactful solutions. Technology alone delivers no value; it takes people with vision to use it. We build the platform—and the Department has the talent to put it to work. We're excited for the pace of innovation ahead.

Headquartered in Bryn Athyn, Pennsylvania, Athyn Insights is here to provide sound, science-based data solutions for your organization's needs. Whether you're a government contractor, business consultant, or startup, we will provide our niche expertise for your field.
