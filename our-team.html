Lana Tang

Owner & Founder

Lana Tang is the founder and owner of Athyn Insights, bringing an entrepreneurial spirit and deep commitment to empowering people through technology. With experience spanning knowledge management, project accounting, and event coordination, Lana has consistently focused on connecting people with the information and tools they need to succeed.

Her entrepreneurial journey includes founding and operating her own floral design business, where she learned firsthand what it takes to build a company from the ground up. At Gensler, she served as a "human search engine"—connecting teams across six Asia offices and creating frameworks that made knowledge accessible and actionable. This people-first approach, combined with her experience in financial management and project coordination, informs Athyn Insights' mission to democratize data science tools and help government professionals realize their full potential.

Lana's greatest passion is helping other people realize their talents. She founded Athyn Insights in 2021 with the vision that cutting-edge data science and AI tools should be accessible to those who need them most, not just those with technical backgrounds.
