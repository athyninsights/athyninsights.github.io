Our Services

Comprehensive Data Science and AI Solutions

We deliver end-to-end data science and AI solutions tailored to the unique needs of the U.S. Department of State and other government agencies. Our services span from platform development to training and empowerment.

We deliver platforms. We build and maintain scalable data platforms that bring AI and data science tools directly to users. Our platforms integrate with enterprise systems like SharePoint and Graph APIs, Azure AI Services, OpenAI, Databricks, and government cloud infrastructure, providing unified interfaces that make complex tools accessible.

We also integrate secure package repositories and enterprise tools for secrets management, budget controls, and workflow automation. We understand what it takes to build platforms that people actually use, not just deploy.

Everything that can be automated must be automated. We automate the simple things so we can focus on the more difficult challenges. Our philosophy is code over SOPs—we build automated solutions rather than writing procedures that require manual execution.

This approach frees up time and resources for the complex problems that truly need human expertise.

We build dashboards and analytical tools that people actually use. We can build container-based Flask or Streamlit apps, or utilize Power Apps as a frontend. Our team works with Splunk, Power BI, and custom Python visualizations to turn raw data into insights that drive decisions.

We code solutions tailored to State's specific challenges. This includes unified interfaces for generative AI, document analysis, and automated workflows. We develop agentic AI applications utilizing Azure AI Services, OpenAI models, and popular frameworks like LangChain and LangGraph to create intelligent, autonomous systems that can reason, plan, and execute complex tasks.

Harnessing the power of GenAI Computer Vision, Document Intelligence, AI Speech, Vector Embedding, RAG, and more, we develop impactful solutions that transform how the Department works with data. Our work spans everything from web scraping with BeautifulSoup to natural language processing with TensorFlow, from regression models to neural networks. We leverage Azure AI Services and OpenAI models to deliver enterprise-grade AI solutions that meet government security and compliance requirements.

We understand the complex budgetary landscape in government contracting. Our platforms include comprehensive cost tracking and budget management features that provide real-time visibility into AI service usage, spending patterns, and resource allocation.

Built-in budget controls with auto-cutoff capabilities prevent cost overruns, while granular logging enables detailed cost attribution and reporting. This isn't just about tracking—it's about giving program managers the tools they need to make informed decisions in a complex funding environment.

We run custom DoS Data Science bootcamps and provide ongoing support through weekly office hours (three sessions per week, with consistent participation). Our training is hands-on and DoS-focused—we teach the tools, but we also teach how to apply them to State's actual problems.

We complement in-person training with video tutorials that enable self-paced learning and provide reference materials for ongoing skill development.

We understand government security requirements. We know that the hardest part of success in government is navigating complex cybersecurity governance processes—we don't shy away from this. Our platforms include built-in cost controls, logging, and enterprise authentication—built for production use in government environments.
